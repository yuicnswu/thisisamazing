**APA:** Arora, N., Chakraborty, I., & Nishimura, Y. (2025). AI-human hybrids for marketing research: Leveraging large language models (LLMs) as collaborators. *Journal of Marketing, 89*(2), 43–70. https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241276529

## 1. Research question
Where in the marketing-research process can LLMs collaborate with human researchers, and how good are the results?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Claims about LLMs in market research were anecdotal and treated LLMs as replacements rather than collaborators.

## 3. Contributions
A framework and studies of LLMs in qualitative and quantitative research stages, with a field application at a firm.

## 4. Proposed methods
Compared LLM and human outputs on interview design, synthetic respondents and analysis of qualitative data.

## 5. Key findings
Hybrid AI-human work gave efficiency and quality gains, while purely synthetic respondents showed limits; humans remain needed for judgement. Won the 2025 AMA/MSI H. Paul Root Award.

## 6. Limitations
Specific tasks and firm; LLM capabilities move quickly.
