**APA:** Hong, Y., & Pavlou, P. A. (2014). Product fit uncertainty in online markets: Nature, effects, and antecedents. *Information Systems Research, 25*(2), 328–344.

## 1. Research question
Is uncertainty about whether a product fits the buyer distinct from uncertainty about its quality, and what are its effects?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Online-market research focused on quality uncertainty and seller reputation.

## 3. Contributions
Defines product fit uncertainty, separates it from quality uncertainty, and identifies its antecedents.

## 4. Proposed methods
Survey and transaction data from online buyers, with structural equation and econometric analysis.

## 5. Key findings
Fit uncertainty is distinct from quality uncertainty, lowers satisfaction and raises returns; product descriptions and similar information reduce it.

## 6. Limitations
Self-reported measures; does not operationalize fit at the level of specific needs.

## 7. Author names
Yili Hong, Paul A. Pavlou
