**APA:** Tirunillai, S., & Tellis, G. J. (2014). Mining marketing meaning from online chatter: Strategic brand analysis of big data using latent Dirichlet allocation. *Journal of Marketing Research, 51*(4), 463–479. https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.12.0106

## 1. Research question
Can topic models on review text extract the dimensions of quality consumers care about, and track them across brands and time?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Quality dimensions came from expert ratings or surveys, which are static and narrow.

## 3. Contributions
A unified LDA framework that extracts quality dimensions and their valence from reviews and maps brands on them.

## 4. Proposed methods
LDA on several hundred thousand reviews across many firms and markets, validated against Consumer Reports dimensions.

## 5. Key findings
Recovered quality dimensions that matched expert ones and showed how their importance varies by market and changes over time.

## 6. Limitations
Bag-of-words topics need interpretation; no causal link to sales.
