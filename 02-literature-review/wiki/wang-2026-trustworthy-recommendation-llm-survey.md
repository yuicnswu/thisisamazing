## 1. Research question
What are the opportunities and challenges for trustworthy LLM-based recommendation?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Trustworthy-recommendation surveys predate LLMs or cover single dimensions.

## 3. Contributions
Reviews over 200 studies across robustness, bias and fairness, explainability, factuality, controllability and privacy; 13 opportunities and 18 challenges.

## 4. Proposed methods
Structured literature survey with datasets and metrics.

## 5. Key findings
Factuality (grounding, hallucinated items) and robustness (poisoning, textual attacks) are central risks; evaluation protocols are immature.

## 6. Limitations
