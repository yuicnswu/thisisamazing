## 1. Research question
How should design science contributions be positioned and written so reviewers see their knowledge contribution?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Design science papers struggled to state what knowledge they add beyond a built system.

## 3. Contributions
The knowledge contribution framework (improvement, invention, exaptation, routine design) and a publication schema.

## 4. Proposed methods
Conceptual framework with examples.

## 5. Key findings
Contributions should be positioned by solution and application maturity; routine design is not a research contribution.
