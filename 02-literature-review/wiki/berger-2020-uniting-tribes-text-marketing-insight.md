**APA:** Berger, J., Humphreys, A., Ludwig, S., Moe, W. W., Netzer, O., & Schweidel, D. A. (2020). Uniting the tribes: Using text for marketing insight. *Journal of Marketing, 84*(1), 1–25. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022242919873106

## 1. Research question
How can text analysis serve marketing research across sub-fields, and what are its pitfalls?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Text methods were scattered across consumer behaviour, strategy and modelling, with uneven validation.

## 3. Contributions
A framework of text as reflecting producers and affecting receivers, a review of methods, and guidance on validity and pitfalls.

## 4. Proposed methods
Conceptual review and methodological guide.

## 5. Key findings
Text offers insight into consumers, firms and markets, but requires careful construct validity and attention to measurement error.

## 6. Limitations
Written before LLMs; guidance on generative models is absent.
