## 1. Research question
How can LLM-based recommenders be organized, and what are their strengths and gaps?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
No unified view of discriminative versus generative uses of LLMs in recommendation.

## 3. Contributions
Divides LLM-based recommendation into discriminative and generative paradigms and reviews each.

## 4. Proposed methods
Structured literature survey.

## 5. Key findings
Generative LLM recommenders are promising but face hallucination, bias, evaluation and efficiency problems.

## 6. Limitations
Little on new-item evidence or abstention.
