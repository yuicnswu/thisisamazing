## 1. Research question
What kinds of knowledge conflict affect LLMs, why do they arise, and how can they be handled?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Conflict studies were scattered across QA, retrieval augmentation and model editing.

## 3. Contributions
A taxonomy of context–memory, inter-context and intra-memory conflicts, with causes, behaviours and solutions.

## 4. Proposed methods
Structured literature survey.

## 5. Key findings
Models behave inconsistently under conflict; solutions include faithful decoding, training and abstaining or flagging conflict.

## 6. Limitations
QA and generation; missing evidence is not a separate case; no recommendation setting.
