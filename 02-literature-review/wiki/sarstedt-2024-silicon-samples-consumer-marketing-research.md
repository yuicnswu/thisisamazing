## 1. Research question
When can LLM-generated 'silicon samples' be used in consumer research, and with what safeguards?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Studies on LLM respondents gave mixed results without guidance on when to trust them.

## 3. Contributions
A review of evidence on silicon samples and guidelines for their use across research stages.

## 4. Proposed methods
Literature review and synthesis.

## 5. Key findings
Silicon samples help in early stages such as pretesting, but are not reliable substitutes for human samples in final inference.

## 6. Limitations
Review of a fast-moving area.
