**APA:** Babić Rosario, A., Sotgiu, F., De Valck, K., & Bijmolt, T. H. A. (2016). The effect of electronic word of mouth on sales: A meta-analytic review of platform, product, and metric factors. *Journal of Marketing Research, 53*(3), 297–318. https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.14.0380

## 1. Research question
How strongly is electronic word of mouth related to sales, and what moderates the relationship?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Individual studies reported conflicting eWOM effects.

## 3. Contributions
Meta-analysis of 96 studies on eWOM and sales, with platform, product and metric moderators.

## 4. Proposed methods
Meta-analytic review.

## 5. Key findings
eWOM correlates positively with sales; the effect varies by platform, product type and metric, and volume matters more than valence.

## 6. Limitations
Correlational; does not study who writes the first reviews.

## 7. Author names
