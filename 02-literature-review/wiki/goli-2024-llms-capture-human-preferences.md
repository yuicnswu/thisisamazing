**APA:** Goli, A., & Singh, A. (2024). Frontiers: Can large language models capture human preferences? *Marketing Science, 43*(4), 709–722. https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0306

## 1. Research question
Do LLM responses reflect human preferences, here intertemporal choice, well enough for research?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Enthusiasm for LLM respondents outran tests of whether their preferences match humans'.

## 3. Contributions
A test of LLM time preferences against human benchmarks, across languages and prompts.

## 4. Proposed methods
Intertemporal choice tasks posed to GPT models in several languages, compared with human data.

## 5. Key findings
LLM preferences were not aligned with humans (more patient than people) and varied by language; chain-of-thought explanations did not fix this.

## 6. Limitations
One preference domain; specific model versions.
