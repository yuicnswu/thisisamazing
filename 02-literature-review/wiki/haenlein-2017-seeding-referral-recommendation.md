**APA:** Haenlein, M., & Libai, B. (2017). Seeding, referral, and recommendation: Creating profitable word-of-mouth programs. *California Management Review, 59*(2), 68–91.

## 1. Research question
How should firms design profitable word-of-mouth programmes?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Seeding, referral and recommendation programmes were studied separately.

## 3. Contributions
A synthesis of research on three WOM programme types and when each is profitable.

## 4. Proposed methods
Review article.

## 5. Key findings
Programme value depends on whom the firm activates and on separating acceleration from true expansion.

## 6. Limitations
Judges programmes by reach and sales, not by the reviews that seeded customers write.

## 7. Author names
Michael Haenlein, Barak Libai
