## 1. Research question
How are LLMs being used to strengthen recommender systems, and what remains open?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Earlier surveys covered pre-trained language models or deep recommenders but not the LLM paradigm.

## 3. Contributions
A taxonomy of LLM-based recommendation by pre-training, fine-tuning and prompting, with future directions.

## 4. Proposed methods
Structured literature survey.

## 5. Key findings
LLMs bring language understanding, generalization and reasoning to recommendation; hallucination, trustworthiness and efficiency are open problems.

## 6. Limitations
Accuracy-oriented; little on whether recommendations are supported by item evidence.
