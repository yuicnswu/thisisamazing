**APA:** Li, P., Castelo, N., Katona, Z., & Sarvary, M. (2024). Frontiers: Determining the validity of large language models for automated perceptual analysis. *Marketing Science, 43*(2), 254–266. https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0454

## 1. Research question
Can LLMs produce brand-perception data (perceptual maps) that agree with human surveys?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Perceptual maps need costly consumer surveys.

## 3. Contributions
A method that prompts LLMs for brand similarity and attribute judgements and builds perceptual maps from them, with a validity test against human data.

## 4. Proposed methods
Compared LLM-derived maps with human survey maps across categories.

## 5. Key findings
LLM-based perceptual maps agreed closely with human survey maps, suggesting LLMs can replace some survey-based market research at low cost.

## 6. Limitations
Well-known brands that appear in training data; may not hold for new products or niche categories.
