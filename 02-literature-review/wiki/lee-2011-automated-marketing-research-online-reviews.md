**APA:** Lee, T. Y., & Bradlow, E. T. (2011). Automated marketing research using online customer reviews. *Journal of Marketing Research, 48*(5), 881–894. https://doi.org/10.1509/jmkr.48.5.881

## 1. Research question
Can online reviews be processed automatically to give the market-structure insights that traditional marketing research provides?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Surveys and interviews are slow and costly; review text was too large to read by hand.

## 3. Contributions
An automated method that turns pros-and-cons review text into product attributes and market-structure analysis.

## 4. Proposed methods
Text mining of review phrases into attribute groups, applied to digital cameras, and compared with conventional marketing research outputs.

## 5. Key findings
Reviews surfaced attributes and competitive structure consistent with, and sometimes beyond, traditional research.

## 6. Limitations
Semi-structured pros/cons text; one category; pre-LLM methods.
