**APA:** Nestaas, F., Debenedetti, E., & Tramèr, F. (2025). Adversarial search engine optimization for large language models. In *International Conference on Learning Representations*.

## 1. Research question
Can third-party content manipulate which products or sources LLM-powered search selects?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Prompt-injection work focused on hijacking assistants, not on economic incentives to promote one's own content.

## 3. Contributions
Preference manipulation attacks; evidence on production systems; a game-theoretic view showing a prisoner's dilemma among content providers.

## 4. Proposed methods
Craft web pages and plugin descriptions that steer LLM search and plugin selection, tested on real systems.

## 5. Key findings
Attacks raise the selection of the attacker's product and, once some parties attack, all have an incentive to attack, degrading results.

## 6. Limitations
Focuses on attacks; defences are discussed but not solved.

## 7. Author names
Fredrik Nestaas, Edoardo Debenedetti, Florian Tramèr
