**APA:** Hu, M., & Liu, B. (2004). Mining and summarizing customer reviews. In *Proceedings of the 10th ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining* (pp. 168–177). https://doi.org/10.1145/1014052.1014073

## 1. Research question
How can many reviews be summarised into product features with counts of positive and negative opinions?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Reading every review is infeasible, and plain text summarisation loses feature-level opinion.

## 3. Contributions
Feature-based review summarisation: mine product features, find opinion sentences and their polarity, and summarise counts per feature.

## 4. Proposed methods
Association mining for frequent features, WordNet-based opinion word polarity, evaluated on electronics reviews.

## 5. Key findings
Effective feature and opinion extraction, establishing aspect-based opinion summarisation.

## 6. Limitations
Lexicon methods miss implicit features, sarcasm and context.
