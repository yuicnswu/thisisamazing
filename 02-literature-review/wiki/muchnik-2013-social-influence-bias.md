**APA:** Muchnik, L., Aral, S., & Taylor, S. J. (2013). Social influence bias: A randomized experiment. *Science, 341*(6146), 647–651.

## 1. Research question
Do prior ratings causally bias later ratings?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Observational rating data cannot separate social influence from true quality.

## 3. Contributions
A large randomized field experiment on herding in online ratings.

## 4. Proposed methods
On a social news site, randomly give new comments an up-vote, a down-vote or no vote, and follow later ratings.

## 5. Key findings
One early up-vote raised the chance of further up-votes and produced a lasting increase in final ratings; negative manipulation was largely corrected by the crowd.

## 6. Limitations
One website and comment ratings, not product reviews.

Lev Muchnik, Sinan Aral, Sean J. Taylor
