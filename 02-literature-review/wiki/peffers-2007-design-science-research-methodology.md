**APA:** Peffers, K., Tuunanen, T., Rothenberger, M. A., & Chatterjee, S. (2007). A design science research methodology for information systems research. *Journal of Management Information Systems, 24*(3), 45–77. https://doi.org/10.2753/MIS0742-1222240302

## 1. Research question
What process should design science research follow?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Design science had guidelines but no commonly accepted process model.

## 3. Contributions
The DSRM process: identify problem, define objectives, design and develop, demonstrate, evaluate, communicate.

## 4. Proposed methods
Synthesis of prior process models, demonstrated with four case studies.

## 5. Key findings
A six-activity process model that fits diverse design science projects.

## 6. Limitations
Process guidance only; does not prescribe evaluation designs.

## 7. Author names
Ken Peffers, Tuure Tuunanen, Marcus A. Rothenberger, Samir Chatterjee

## 8. Publish year
2007
