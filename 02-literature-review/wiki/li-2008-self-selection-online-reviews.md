**APA:** Li, X., & Hitt, L. M. (2008). Self-selection and information role of online product reviews. *Information Systems Research, 19*(4), 456–474.

## 1. Research question
Are early product reviews representative of what later buyers will experience?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Work on reviews assumed ratings are unbiased signals of quality.

## 3. Contributions
Shows early reviewers self-select and models how this biases ratings and later demand.

## 4. Proposed methods
A model of consumer learning with heterogeneous preferences, tested on Amazon book reviews over time.

## 5. Key findings
Early buyers have systematically different preferences; ratings trend over time, and the bias affects sales and consumer surplus.

## 6. Limitations
Books; fit between buyer and product is inferred, not measured.
