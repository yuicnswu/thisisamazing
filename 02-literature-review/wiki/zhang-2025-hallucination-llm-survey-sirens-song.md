## 1. Research question
How should hallucination in LLMs be defined, detected, explained and mitigated?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Hallucination work from task-specific NLG did not transfer to general-purpose LLMs.

## 3. Contributions
A taxonomy of input-, context- and fact-conflicting hallucination, with benchmarks, sources and mitigation across the LLM life cycle.

## 4. Proposed methods
Structured literature survey.

## 5. Key findings
Hallucination arises across pre-training, alignment and inference; retrieval and uncertainty-based abstention are among the mitigations.

## 6. Limitations
General; no decision-level or recommendation-specific treatment.
