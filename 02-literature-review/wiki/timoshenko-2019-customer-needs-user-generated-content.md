## 1. Research question
Can user-generated content replace interviews for identifying customer needs?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Needs are traditionally found through costly interviews and focus groups; UGC is large and mostly uninformative.

## 3. Contributions
A machine-learning pipeline that makes UGC efficient for needs discovery, compared directly with interviews.

## 4. Proposed methods
A convolutional network filters uninformative sentences and clustering removes redundancy before human analysts extract needs.

## 5. Key findings
UGC identifies needs at least as well as interviews, and more efficiently.

## 6. Limitations
Still needs human analysts in the last step; one product category.
