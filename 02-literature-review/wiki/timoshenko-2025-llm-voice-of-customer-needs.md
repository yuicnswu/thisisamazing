**APA:** Timoshenko, A., Mao, C., & Hauser, J. R. (2025). *Transforming the voice of the customer: Large language models for identifying customer needs* (arXiv:2503.01870). arXiv.

## 1. Research question
Can LLMs turn raw customer feedback into customer needs as well as professional market-research analysts?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
The 2019 pipeline still needed human analysts to formulate needs from filtered text.

## 3. Contributions
A comparison of professional analysts, prompted foundation models and supervised fine-tuned LLMs on needs extraction across categories.

## 4. Proposed methods
Fine-tune LLMs on analyst-formulated needs; compare outputs with analysts on quality, specificity and grounding.

## 5. Key findings
Fine-tuned LLMs matched or beat professional analysts and clearly beat prompted foundation models; needs were specific and grounded in the source text.

## 6. Limitations
Preprint; analyst labels needed for fine-tuning; extraction of needs, not decisions.
