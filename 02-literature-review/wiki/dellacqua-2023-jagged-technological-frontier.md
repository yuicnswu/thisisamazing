## 1. Research question
How does GPT-4 change the productivity and quality of consultants' knowledge work?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Evidence on generative AI at work came from narrow tasks or lab settings.

## 3. Contributions
A pre-registered field experiment with 758 BCG consultants on realistic tasks inside and outside the AI's capability frontier.

## 4. Proposed methods
Random assignment to no AI, GPT-4, or GPT-4 with prompt training on product-development and business-analysis tasks.

## 5. Key findings
Inside the frontier, consultants with AI finished more tasks, faster and at higher quality; outside it, they were less likely to reach the correct answer.

## 6. Limitations
One firm and short tasks; working paper.
