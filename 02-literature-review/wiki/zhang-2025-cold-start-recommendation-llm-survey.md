## 1. Research question
How has cold-start recommendation developed, and how do LLMs change it?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Earlier cold-start surveys predate LLMs.

## 3. Contributions
A roadmap from content features and graph relations to domain information and LLM world knowledge.

## 4. Proposed methods
Structured literature survey.

## 5. Key findings
LLM world knowledge is presented as a powerful source for cold-start users and items.

## 6. Limitations
Treats LLM knowledge as a solution; does not discuss evaluating whether new-item recommendations are supported by evidence, or abstention. Preprint.
