**APA:** Fleder, D., & Hosanagar, K. (2009). Blockbuster culture's next rise or fall: The impact of recommender systems on sales diversity. *Management Science, 55*(5), 697–712.

## 1. Research question
Do recommender systems increase or decrease the diversity of what people buy?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Recommenders were assumed to help niche products reach buyers (the long tail), without formal analysis.

## 3. Contributions
An analytical and simulation model of recommender feedback on aggregate sales concentration.

## 4. Proposed methods
Model consumers choosing with and without common recommender designs, and simulate over time.

## 5. Key findings
Common recommenders can reduce aggregate diversity even while individuals discover new items, because they rely on past sales.

## 6. Limitations
Stylized model; predates LLM recommenders.

Daniel Fleder, Kartik Hosanagar
