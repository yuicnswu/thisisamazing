## 1. Research question
How can we judge whether generated text is supported by an identified source?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Faithfulness was measured inconsistently across tasks, without a shared definition of support.

## 3. Contributions
The Attributable to Identified Sources (AIS) framework: a definition of attribution and a two-stage human annotation protocol.

## 4. Proposed methods
Annotators first judge whether a statement is interpretable, then whether 'according to the source, ...' holds; applied to QA, summarisation and dialogue.

## 5. Key findings
AIS can be annotated reliably and exposes unsupported content that fluency metrics miss.

## 6. Limitations
Human annotation is costly; the framework judges support, not truth or usefulness.
