**APA:** Netzer, O., Feldman, R., Goldenberg, J., & Fresko, M. (2012). Mine your own business: Market-structure surveillance through text mining. *Marketing Science, 31*(3), 521–543. https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.1120.0713

## 1. Research question
Can user-generated forum text reveal how consumers perceive market structure and brand associations?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Market-structure studies used sales or survey data, which are costly and miss consumer language.

## 3. Contributions
A text-mining pipeline from forum posts to brand co-occurrence maps and brand-attribute associations.

## 4. Proposed methods
Entity and term extraction, co-occurrence networks and comparison with sales and survey data, in sedans and diabetes drugs.

## 5. Key findings
Text-based maps matched sales- and survey-based structure and revealed associations such as side effects that other data missed.

## 6. Limitations
Forum data and older NLP; co-occurrence is not sentiment or causation.
