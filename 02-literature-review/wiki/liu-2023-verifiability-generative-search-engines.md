**APA:** Liu, N. F., Zhang, T., & Liang, P. (2023). Evaluating verifiability in generative search engines. In *Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2023* (pp. 7001–7025).

## 1. Research question
Are the answers of commercial generative search engines actually supported by the sources they cite?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Users trust cited answers, but citation recall and precision had not been audited.

## 3. Contributions
A human audit of four generative search engines with citation recall (sentences fully supported) and citation precision (citations that support their sentence).

## 4. Proposed methods
Annotators judged support for each sentence-citation pair across diverse queries.

## 5. Key findings
Only about half of generated sentences were fully supported, and about a quarter of citations did not support their sentence; fluent, useful-looking answers were often less verifiable.

## 6. Limitations
Systems change quickly; human audit at one time point.
