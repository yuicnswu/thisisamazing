**APA:** Allouah, A., Besbes, O., Figueroa, J. D., Kanoria, Y., & Kumar, A. (2025). *What is your AI agent buying? Evaluation, biases, model dependence, & emerging implications for agentic e-commerce* (arXiv:2508.02630). arXiv.

## 1. Research question
How do AI shopping agents choose products in a marketplace, and what biases and instabilities shape those choices?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Agentic shopping was being deployed without audits of how agents pick products or how sellers can influence them.

## 3. Contributions
ACES, a provider-agnostic audit framework with randomised trials, plus a seller-side agent that edits descriptions.

## 4. Proposed methods
Randomise product attributes, positions and tags in a mock marketplace; observe agent choices across providers and model versions; let a seller agent make query-conditional description edits.

## 5. Key findings
Agents concentrate demand on a few products, shares shift sharply after model updates, position bias is strong, sponsored tags are penalised and platform endorsements rewarded; simple query-specific description edits raise a seller's market share significantly.

## 6. Limitations
Specific providers and versions; simple tasks with a clear best choice.
