**APA:** Brynjolfsson, E., Li, D., & Raymond, L. (2025). Generative AI at work. *The Quarterly Journal of Economics, 140*(2), 889–942. https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044

## 1. Research question
What happens to worker productivity when a generative AI assistant is deployed in a real firm?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Little causal evidence existed on generative AI in real workplaces.

## 3. Contributions
Staggered roll-out evidence from customer-support agents at a large firm.

## 4. Proposed methods
Difference-in-differences on the staggered introduction of an AI conversational assistant.

## 5. Key findings
Productivity rose by about 14% on average and much more for novice and low-skilled workers, with little effect on the most experienced.

## 6. Limitations
One firm and one task type (customer support).

## 7. Author names
Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li, Lindsey Raymond
