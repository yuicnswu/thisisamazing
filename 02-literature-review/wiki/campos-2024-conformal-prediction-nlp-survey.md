## 1. Research question
How has conformal prediction been applied in NLP, and what are the open challenges?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Uncertainty methods for NLP rarely give finite-sample guarantees.

## 3. Contributions
Reviews conformal techniques, guarantees and NLP applications, including LLM hallucination.

## 4. Proposed methods
Structured literature survey.

## 5. Key findings
Conformal methods suit NLP because they are model-agnostic; exchangeability, conditional coverage and generation are key challenges.

## 6. Limitations
No conjunctive decision guarantees; no recommendation.
