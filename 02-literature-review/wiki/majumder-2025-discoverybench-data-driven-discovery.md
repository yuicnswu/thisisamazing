## 1. Research question
Can LLMs automate the search for and verification of hypotheses from given datasets?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
No benchmark formalised multi-step data-driven discovery with real published workflows across domains.

## 3. Contributions
A benchmark of real discovery tasks drawn from published papers in six domains (including sociology, economics and marketing-adjacent fields), plus synthetic tasks, and a structured hypothesis-matching evaluation.

## 4. Proposed methods
Each task gives datasets, metadata and a goal; systems produce a hypothesis that is scored against the gold hypothesis by context, variables and relationship.

## 5. Key findings
Even the best agent frameworks solve only a small fraction of tasks (the paper reports about 25% at best), showing that autonomous discovery is far from solved.

## 6. Limitations
Gold hypotheses come from one published analysis per task, so valid alternative findings can be scored as wrong.
