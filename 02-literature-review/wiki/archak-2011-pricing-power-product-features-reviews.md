**APA:** Archak, N., Ghose, A., & Ipeirotis, P. G. (2011). Deriving the pricing power of product features by mining consumer reviews. *Management Science, 57*(8), 1485–1509. https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.1110.1370

## 1. Research question
Can text mining of reviews reveal which product features, and which opinions about them, drive sales?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Demand models used star ratings and could not see which features reviewers discussed.

## 3. Contributions
Combines text mining of feature–opinion pairs with econometric demand models.

## 4. Proposed methods
Extract product features and evaluative phrases from Amazon reviews and include them in demand estimation.

## 5. Key findings
Opinions about specific features affect demand and pricing power, and the text predicts sales beyond ratings.

## 6. Limitations
Older NLP; feature extraction is noisy.
