**APA:** Godes, D., & Silva, J. C. (2012). Sequential and temporal dynamics of online opinion. *Marketing Science, 31*(3), 448–473.

## 1. Research question
Why do average online ratings decline as products accumulate reviews?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Earlier work linked the decline to time alone and could not separate the effect of time from the effect of review order.

## 3. Contributions
Separates sequential (order) and temporal (time) dynamics of ratings and explains the decline.

## 4. Proposed methods
Panel models of Amazon book reviews that include both review order and calendar time.

## 5. Key findings
Ratings fall with the number of prior reviews, consistent with later buyers being less well matched to the product.

## 6. Limitations
Books; mismatch inferred from patterns, not observed.
