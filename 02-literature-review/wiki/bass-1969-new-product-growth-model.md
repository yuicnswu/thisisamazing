**APA:** Bass, F. M. (1969). A new product growth for model consumer durables. *Management Science, 15*(5), 215–227. https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.15.5.215

## 1. Research question
How does the timing of first purchases of a new product unfold over time?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Earlier diffusion models described adoption curves without separating external influence from imitation among adopters.

## 3. Contributions
The Bass diffusion model, with a coefficient of innovation and a coefficient of imitation.

## 4. Proposed methods
A hazard model of first purchase as a function of innovation and the share of earlier adopters, estimated on sales of eleven consumer durables.

## 5. Key findings
The model fits the sales curves well and predicts the timing and size of peak sales.

## 6. Limitations
Aggregate model; ignores who the adopters are, product quality, repeat purchase and marketing actions.
