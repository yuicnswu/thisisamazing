## 1. Research question
Do cognitive-bias cues in product descriptions (for example social proof or scarcity) shift LLM recommendations?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Adversarial attacks on recommenders are usually detectable; subtle persuasive edits that look like normal marketing were not studied.

## 3. Contributions
A study of cognitive-bias edits as a hard-to-detect manipulation of LLM recommenders, across models.

## 4. Proposed methods
Insert bias-framed phrases into product descriptions and measure the change in recommendation rate; test defensive prompts.

## 5. Key findings
Small bias edits significantly change product visibility, and defensive prompting does not reliably stop this.

## 6. Limitations
Measures injected cues; does not study missing evidence or offer a structural defence.
