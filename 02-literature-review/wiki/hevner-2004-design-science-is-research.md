**APA:** Hevner, A. R., March, S. T., Park, J., & Ram, S. (2004). Design science in information systems research. *MIS Quarterly, 28*(1), 75–105. https://doi.org/10.2307/25148625

## 1. Research question
How should IS researchers build and evaluate IT artifacts as a form of research?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
IS research favoured behavioural science; building artifacts lacked accepted standards of rigour.

## 3. Contributions
Seven guidelines for design science research (artifact, relevance, evaluation, contribution, rigour, search, communication).

## 4. Proposed methods
Conceptual framework illustrated with exemplar studies.

## 5. Key findings
Design science is a legitimate research paradigm when artifacts are rigorously evaluated and contribute to the knowledge base.

## 6. Limitations
Guidelines are high level; evaluation methods are left open.

## 7. Author names
Alan R. Hevner, Salvatore T. March, Jinsoo Park, Sudha Ram
