## 1. Research question
When and how should LLMs abstain, and how is abstention evaluated?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Abstention research was split between refusal for safety and uncertainty for correctness.

## 3. Contributions
A framework that views abstention from the query, the model and human values; a review of methods, benchmarks and metrics; open directions.

## 4. Proposed methods
Structured literature survey.

## 5. Key findings
Abstention is under-studied as a general capability; evaluation metrics are inconsistent.

## 6. Limitations
No recommendation setting and no distribution-free guarantee.
