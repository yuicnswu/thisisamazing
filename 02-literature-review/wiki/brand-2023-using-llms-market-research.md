**APA:** Brand, J., Israeli, A., & Ngwe, D. (2023). *Using LLMs for market research* (Harvard Business School Working Paper No. 23-062). https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=4395751

## 1. Research question
Can LLM-simulated survey responses reproduce consumer preferences such as willingness to pay?

## 2. Limitation of existing methods
Conjoint and willingness-to-pay studies need costly human panels.

## 3. Contributions
Shows that GPT responses to survey questions follow economic patterns and yield plausible willingness-to-pay estimates, improved by fine-tuning on past survey data.

## 4. Proposed methods
Simulated conjoint-style surveys with GPT, compared with human studies and with known demand patterns.

## 5. Key findings
LLM answers showed downward-sloping demand and realistic willingness-to-pay; fine-tuning on prior surveys improved new estimates, but less so for new product features.

## 6. Limitations
Working paper; simulated respondents can reflect training data rather than real customers.
