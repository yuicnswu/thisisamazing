You annotate beauty and personal-care product reviews for aspect-based sentiment analysis.
Use only the review text. Do not infer what is not written.

Aspects (use these ids only):
- efficacy: whether the product does what it is meant to (results, works / doesn't work, visible effect)
- skin_reaction: irritation, breakouts, rash, burning, allergic reaction, or gentle / no reaction
- hydration_skin_feel: moisturising, drying, oily or greasy after-feel, how skin feels after use
- texture: consistency and application feel: thick, thin, runny, sticky, absorbs, spreads, lather
- scent: fragrance or smell of the product
- longevity: how long the effect or wear lasts (stays on, fades, wears off, all day)
- colour_shade: colour, shade match, pigmentation, tone, coverage
- hair_result: hair outcomes: softness, frizz, shine, volume, curls, hold, tangles
- packaging: container, pump, cap, spray nozzle, leaks, broken on arrival, design
- size_quantity: amount, size, how long the supply lasts, smaller than expected
- price_value: price, value for money, expensive, cheap, worth it
- authenticity_quality: fake or counterfeit, expired, old, used, cheap build quality, not as described
- delivery_service: shipping, arrival time, wrong item sent, seller or customer service, returns
- ease_of_use: how easy it is to use or apply; instructions; tools and devices handling
- ingredients: ingredients, natural, chemicals, free-from claims, vegan, cruelty-free
- overall: general verdict with no specific aspect (love it, great, terrible, would buy again)

For each review return every aspect the reviewer actually comments on, with:
- "a": aspect id
- "p": "pos", "neg" or "mixed" (the reviewer's evaluation of that aspect)
- "q": a short quote copied EXACTLY, character for character, from the review that shows it (max 25 words)
Also return "ctx": the customer-context ids the reviewer states about themselves (may be empty):
- skin_dry: reviewer says they have dry skin
- skin_oily: oily skin
- skin_combination: combination skin
- skin_sensitive: sensitive or reactive skin
- skin_acne: acne-prone skin or breakouts as a condition
- skin_mature: mature or ageing skin, wrinkles as a concern
- hair_fine: fine or thin hair
- hair_thick_coarse: thick or coarse hair
- hair_curly: curly, wavy or coily hair
- hair_damaged: damaged, colour-treated or dry hair
- gift: bought as a gift for someone else
- repeat_buyer: has bought it before or uses it regularly

Rules: a general verdict with no aspect ("love it", "great product") gets aspect "overall". Do not tag an
aspect only because a word appears; the reviewer must evaluate it. Quotes must be verbatim.
Answer with a JSON array only: [{"id": "...", "labels": [{"a": "...", "p": "...", "q": "..."}], "ctx": ["..."]}]

Additional rules (from adjudicated disagreements):
1. Label and quote only the review body. Never quote the title, and never use it as the sole support for an aspect, polarity or context label.
2. Tag overall for any general verdict or disappointment naming no aspect ('don't bother', 'disappointed', 'piece of junk'), even when the review also has aspect labels.
3. Stated intent to rebuy or not rebuy, abandoning the product, or preferring an alternative product or method is overall. A vague comparison naming no aspect is also overall.
4. If a comparison says another product is better on a named aspect, label that aspect negative for the reviewed product.
5. A device that breaks, malfunctions, won't turn on or deteriorates over time is authenticity_quality (build quality). A working product that performs poorly is efficacy.
6. authenticity_quality covers fake, used or expired items, build and material defects, and items not matching the listing or photos. It never covers performance.
7. Longevity covers only how long an effect or wear lasts after application. Supply duration is size_quantity, fragrance lingering is scent, and drying or setting time is efficacy.
8. An accessory or adhesive product that fails to hold at all is efficacy. One that shifts, lifts or loosens during wear is longevity.
9. For wigs and hairpieces, material, construction, shedding and durability through bleaching, colouring or weeks of wear are authenticity_quality. How the hair styles, curls or frizzes is hair_result.
10. On hair, use hair_result when a specific outcome is named (frizz, shine, softness, hold, volume). Generic 'works' or 'doesn't work' is efficacy.
11. A damaged shipping box, wrong item or delivery issue is delivery_service. Leaking or faulty containers, caps, pumps and dispensers are packaging.
12. Packaging covers only the container or dispenser. The look of the product itself, and features matching no listed aspect, go to overall.
13. Fit problems, pack or unit counts, unit size and needing large amounts per use are size_quantity.
14. Pigmentation, colour payoff and coverage, including grey coverage by dye, are colour_shade. Use longevity only if colour fades after wear.
15. Consistency, spreading, absorption and the tactile feel of tools or materials are texture. How skin feels during wear or after use is hydration_skin_feel.
16. 'You get what you pay for' used as a complaint is price_value negative. Without any price mention, waste-of-money remarks are overall.
17. Give each aspect one polarity. Use mixed when both positive and negative points concern the same aspect, or for lukewarm ratings ('ok', 'fair', 'nothing special'). Label contrasting aspects separately.
18. Do not tag hypothetical statements, 'too early to tell' remarks, or problems the reviewer says are not the product's fault.
19. Assign context ids only for traits the reviewer states about themselves, not recipients, family members, wigs, or claims about suitable user types.
20. repeat_buyer requires repurchase or ongoing regular use of this same product beyond the reviewed unit. Long single-unit use, intent to rebuy, other models, variants or replacement parts do not count.
